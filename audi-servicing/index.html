Audi know-how

Where Audis need an experienced eye

These are the jobs that separate a proper Audi service from an oil change. We check for each one when your car's on the hoist.

Oil use on TFSI engines

Earlier 2.0 TFSI · A3, A4, A5, Q3, Q5, about 2008 to 2012
SignsTopping up oil between services.
Our approachThe usual cause is the piston rings. Regular top-ups hide the problem, so we'd rather diagnose it early and give you the options.

Timing chain tensioner

Same TFSI engine family
SignsA rattle for a second or two on a cold start.
Our approachThat's the early warning. A chain that jumps a tooth means engine repairs, so we check it and quote the fix before it gets there.

S tronic gearbox servicing

Dual-clutch automatics
SignsA shudder moving off in slow traffic, or no record of a gearbox service.
Our approachWet-clutch units need fluid and filter at the logbook interval, commonly around 60,000 km. It's the job most often skipped after the dealer years. We confirm which unit you have and what's due.

Warning lights

Every modern Audi
SignsAn amber light on the dash, or the car dropping into limp mode.
Our approachDealer-level diagnostics read the actual fault codes across the car's systems. We charge for diagnosis, and you decide on the repair once you've heard the result.

Credentials, explained

Why an independent can look after your Audi properly

  1. We're a licensed repair business. WA law requires any business repairing cars to be licensed and its repairers certified, so there's a regulator standing behind every job.
  2. Your warranty is protected. The ACCC is clear that an independent can service a new car and stamp the logbook, provided the work meets the maker's specification. Extended warranties bought separately can differ, so check yours.
  3. We work from Audi's own information. Since 1 July 2022, car makers must sell repair and service information to independents on fair terms. Our specifications match the dealer's.

If it's not right, we'll make it right

We stand behind the work we do on your Audi. If a repair we've done isn't right, bring the car back and we'll sort it. Parts we fit carry their maker's warranty, and we look after the claim.

FAQ

Common Audi questions

Do you service TDI diesels as well as TFSI petrol engines?

Yes, both. On TDIs we also keep an eye on the EGR system, which can clog on cars that mostly do short trips.

Which models?

A1 to A8, the Q range, TT and the RS models.

My Audi has quattro. Anything different?

Sometimes. Transverse-engine quattro cars like the A3, TT and Q3 use a rear coupling that needs its own oil service, and it often gets missed. We check whether yours is due as part of the service.

What will it cost?

We quote your car specifically and put it in writing before we start.

How long does a service take?

It depends on the job. We'll confirm the timing when you book.
