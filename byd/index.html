What we see on BYDs

Four things worth checking on an electric car

No oil changes on a full EV doesn't mean nothing wears. These are the jobs that tend to get missed once the car stops going back to the dealer.

All BYD models

The 12-volt battery

What you notice: the car won't power on, or warnings pop up after it's sat for a few days.

What we do: test the 12V battery and how it's being charged, look for anything draining it, and replace it if it's on the way out.

Atto 3, Dolphin, Seal, Sealion 7

Brakes that don't get used enough

What you notice: a scrape or grind on the first few stops, or the car pulling slightly when you brake.

What we do: regen braking does most of the stopping, so discs can rust and calipers can stick. We clean, check and service them, and replace the brake fluid when the schedule calls for it.

Heavier EVs and the Shark 6

Tyres

What you notice: tyres wearing faster than you expected, or worn more on one edge.

What we do: rotate them, set the pressures and check how they're wearing. Electric cars are heavy and quick off the mark, which is harder on tyres.

Shark 6 and Sealion 6 plug-in hybrids

The engine you rarely hear

What you notice: nothing, which is the problem. If you mostly drive on battery, the petrol side gets forgotten.

What we do: change the engine oil and filter and do the engine checks on your DM-i schedule, alongside the EV side.

Before you book

Things people ask us

Are you a BYD dealership?

No. We're an independent, family-owned workshop in Shenton Park with no connection to BYD Auto Australia or any of its dealers.

Will servicing with you affect my new-car warranty?

No. Under Australian Consumer Law an independent workshop can service your car and stamp the logbook without affecting the manufacturer's warranty, as long as the work follows BYD's schedule and uses parts and fluids of suitable quality. That's how we do every service. If something does need a warranty claim, that part goes through a BYD dealer.

Do you service the Shark 6?

Yes. It's a plug-in hybrid, so it gets the EV checks and the engine side as well.

How often does a BYD need servicing?

It depends on the model. Full EVs and the DM-i plug-in hybrids run on different schedules, and some have an early first service. Tell us the model and kilometres when you book and we'll work to your handbook.

Can you do recalls?

No, recalls are done by BYD dealers.

What will it cost?

Depends on the model and which service is due, so we quote it for your car and put it in writing before we start. Diagnosis is charged. Any repair after that is your call once you've heard what we found.
