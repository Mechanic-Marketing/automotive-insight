What we see on Toyotas

Four things we check that often get missed

Toyotas go a long way if the small jobs get done on time. These are the ones that tend to slip once the car stops going to the dealer.

Corolla, Camry, RAV4, Kluger and Prius hybrids

Hybrid battery and cooling

What you notice: fuel use creeping up, the petrol engine running more than it used to, or a hybrid system warning on the dash.

What we do: read the battery cell by cell, check the inverter coolant and the battery cooling fan and filter, and give you a straight answer on how the pack is going.

Hilux, Prado and Fortuner 2.8 diesel

DPF and injectors

What you notice: a DPF light, a burning smell after short trips, or the car feeling flat and using more fuel.

What we do: check the soot load and the regeneration history, look at the injectors, and diagnose it properly rather than forcing a regen and hoping.

Prado, LandCruiser and Hilux 4x4

Diff, transfer case and gearbox oils

What you notice: a whine from the back on the freeway, or no sign in the service history that the driveline oils were ever done.

What we do: change them at the intervals Toyota sets. It's a cheap job on a 4WD and it's the one most often skipped.

Corolla, Camry, RAV4 and C-HR automatics and CVTs

Transmission fluid

What you notice: a shudder moving off, a flare between gears, or a car on 100,000 km that's never had the transmission touched.

What we do: service it with the right Toyota fluid and the right procedure. Done on time it's routine. Left too long it gets expensive.

Before you book

Things people ask us

Are you a Toyota dealership?

No. We're an independent, family-owned workshop in Shenton Park. We've got nothing to do with Toyota Motor Corporation Australia or any of its dealers. We service and repair Toyotas because we've been doing it for thirty years and people keep coming back.

Will servicing with you affect my new-car warranty?

No. Under Australian Consumer Law an independent workshop can service your car and stamp the logbook without affecting the manufacturer's warranty, as long as the work follows Toyota's schedule and uses parts of suitable quality. That's how we do every service.

Do you service Toyota hybrids?

Yes. Corolla, Camry, RAV4, Kluger, C-HR and Prius hybrids all come through here. Our technicians are trained on high-voltage systems and we check the hybrid battery as part of the service.

Which Toyotas do you work on?

Pretty much all of them. Yaris, Corolla, Camry, C-HR, RAV4, Kluger, Hilux, Fortuner, Prado and LandCruiser, petrol, diesel or hybrid. If yours isn't on the list, give us a call.

What will it cost?

Depends on the model and which service is due, so we quote it for your car and put it in writing before we start. Diagnosis is charged. Any repair after that is your call once you've heard what we found.
